## Scope and Limits

The patch covers opt-in unsent-text replacement, authorization-time composer
changes, current scoped Send selection and more conservative acknowledgement.
Pause, detected attachments and target identity remain authoritative. Two
independent focused reviews informed the changes and their documented limits.

Local browser fixtures and the cloud headed Chromium suite passed. They do not
prove permanent live-site compatibility, model progress or exactly-once delivery.
Historical content remounted with both a new node and unseen ID remains a DOM-only
ambiguity. Reusing a known node for a new message remains unknown rather than
advancing the queue. See [validation](../../VALIDATION.md) and
[Extension Priority](../COMPOSER-PRIORITY.md).
