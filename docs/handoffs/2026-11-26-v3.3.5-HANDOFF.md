## 3. Hard Safety Invariants (Do Not Regress)

1. **`DEFAULT_CONFIG` opt-ins stay `false`**: `autoAllow: false`, `autoAuthorizeTakeover: false`, `startImmediately: false`, `waitForIdle: false`.
2. **No blind replay of unknown submissions**: only pre-dispatch failures may retry; uncertain or attempted commits still pause cleanly.
3. **CAPTCHA safety boundary**: real CAPTCHA widgets/iframes (`reCAPTCHA`, `hCaptcha`, `Cloudflare Turnstile`, `verify you are human`) always pause (`captcha-required`).
4. **Consecutive takeover cap**: `consecutiveTakeoverReplies` caps at 3 consecutive replies per uninterrupted sequence (`resets to 0` on normal `sent` or explicit `RESUME`).
5. **Privacy**: prompts, queues, custom `takeoverReply`, and DOM text must never enter `logs`, `deliveryDiagnostics`, or `buildActivityExport`.
6. **Public docs links**: `tests/public-documentation-tests.mjs` requires relative links inside `README.md`, `README.zh-CN.md`, `PRIVACY.md`, `docs/STORE-LISTING.en.md`, and `native/bootstrap/README.md` to exist inside their respective ZIP allowlists (use full GitHub URLs otherwise).
