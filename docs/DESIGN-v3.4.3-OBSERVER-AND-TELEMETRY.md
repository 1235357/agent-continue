# Design Document — v3.4.3 Observer Reliability & Incident Telemetry Integrity

> **Revision 8** — Resolves all Revision 7 cold review findings (`comprehension` `87fc611b`, `criticism` `113fcf9f`, `readiness` `5c363887`): eliminates every 2-second infinite loop by reserving `handled: false` strictly for transient `phase === 'sending'` and bounded (`<= 3`) coexisting `auto-allowed-prompt` masking (returning `handled: true` on `ready`, `agent-busy`, `approval-required`, already-handled tokens, `oauth-popup` fallthrough, and `runTakeoverReplyRound` pre-dispatch outcomes), adds full `content.js` <-> `adapter.js` 1:1 parity (`stripClosedSignInLines`, `isDotPage` gates, `controlButtons.length === 1`, `strictCaptchaPattern`, `findApprovalPrompt` overlay-before-slice, `pauseSelector`), serializes orphaned `initialize()` storage writes via `diagnosticStorageWrite`, adds `scanInFlight` mutex + nested `if (kind)` Step 3 + uniform `{ timedOut }` `raceWithTimeout`, and closes remaining visibility/test-harness gaps.

## 1. The Problem

Three cumulative `v3.4.2` production telemetry exports (`agent-continue-activity_(21).json` at 10:31 CST — 56 sends over 9.26h; `agent-continue-activity_(22).json` at 16:09 CST — 89 sends over 14.8h (`spanMs: 53,328,480`), with a 77-send / ~12.7h streak; and the latest 22.5h / **134-send** export (`spanMs: 80,986,134`) with zero unexpected pauses, zero errors, and zero alarm phase drift) exposed three concrete runtime and observability bugs:

1. **`incidentDeliveries` corruption, pre-upgrade retention, and real-incident eviction (`background.js`, `popup.js`, `support.js`)**: `isIncidentTrace(trace)` returns `true` whenever `trace.state !== 'sent'`, which matches in-flight `state === 'running'` intermediate snapshots (on Dot sends, `invoke('prepare')` at `background.js:1717` records `'unsupported-view'` and `L1762` immediately advances `pending.stage = 'dispatch'`, so the queued flush writes `stage: 'dispatch', reason: 'unsupported-view'`). Once delivery completes cleanly (`state: 'sent', reason: 'receipt-confirmed'`), `freshIncidents` is empty, so `incidentDeliveries` is never updated or pruned, leaving 20 frozen `'running'` records (`1003..1022` in export 21; `1036..1055` in export 22; `1083..1102` in the 134-send export) that evict genuine incidents (such as `takeover-replied` at `seq 969` and `seq 1078`). Because `popup.js:952` and `support.js:69` read `incidentDeliveries` directly from `chrome.storage.local`, upgraded `v3.4.2` installations would also retain stale `'running'` records on immediate export before their first `v3.4.3` send unless pruned at initialization as well.
2. **Spurious `unknown-modal` alerts immediately after `started` (`dialog-classifier.js`, `content.js`)**: Five `unknown-modal` events occurred `1.74s–2.67s` (`1737 ms`, `2307 ms`, `2604 ms`, `2625 ms`, `2673 ms`) after `started` despite the 5,000 ms dwell rule because (a) `SCAN_DIALOG_CONTEXT { reset: true }` resets `lastKind` in `content.js` without clearing pre-existing timestamps in `firstSeenMap` or canceling a queued pre-reset scan timer, and (b) `detectUnknownModal()` and `detectOAuthPopup()` omit ancestor and element checks for `opacity: 0`, `visibility: collapse`, dialog `pointer-events: none`, `[inert]`/`[hidden]`/`[aria-hidden="true"]`, closed state (`data-state="closed"`, `data-open="false"`), navigation/sidebar containers, zero-sized portals (`width < 2 || height < 2`), and completely empty non-interactive `[role="dialog"]` shells.
3. **Zero-lag takeover observer misses, observer–adapter split-brain, and misattributed trigger (`content.js`, `adapter.js`, `background.js`)**: Live `takeover-needed` turns (`seq 969` and `seq 1078`) were caught only at the 10-minute `round` poll rather than by `content.js` `observeIdleTakeoverPrompt()` because (a) `document.querySelector(busyControlSelector)` matches hidden/overlay/sidebar `.typing-indicator` or Dot's persistent `Dot Thinking` pill even when no Stop button exists and the main composer displays its Send button (often disabled while the composer is empty), (b) `text.length > 4000` silently drops longer Dot turns ending in confirmation requests, (c) neither `observeIdleTakeoverPrompt()` nor `adapter.js:checkTakeoverConversationPrompt()` enforces trailing user-turn dominance cleanly (and `adapter.js:findDotSignInCard()` queries unfiltered `userTurns`/`btns`), (d) `content.js` relies on a cached `classifyDialogContext` closure, gates `observeIdleTakeoverPrompt()` behind an `else if` branch after `hasUnansweredRoutineToolCard()`, lacks retry recovery when `background.js` returns a transient `phase === 'sending'` rejection (`TAKEOVER_PROMPT_OBSERVED` or delegated `DIALOG_EXCEPTION` `oauth-popup`), (e) `MutationObserver` ignores `characterData`, `'inert'`, `'open'`, `'data-open'`, and visibility/disabled attributes, (f) under `waitForIdle: true` / `checkReplyTurn`, `adapter.js:checkTakeoverConversationPrompt(false)` lets `.typing-indicator` mask conversational takeover turns into `'agent-busy'`, and (g) `finishRoundLocked()` hardcodes `trigger: 'round'` on `takeover-replied` even when initiated by `'observer'`.

## 2. The Technical Plan

The design keeps all existing Schema 5 fields (`activity-export.js`, `delivery-diagnostics.js`) and existing `storage.local.get('deliveryDiagnostics')` single-string test hooks intact across four coordinated modules:

```text
[DOM / Page]
  |-- Dialogs --> dialog-classifier.js: isVisibleBlockingDialog() + pruneHiddenDialogTimers()
  |                 +-- ancestor display/visibility/opacity/hidden/inert/aria-hidden/closed + nav/banner
  |                 +-- pointer-events: none checked on dialog ONLY; display: contents visible child/range rect check
  |                 +-- detectUnknownModal() + top-level pruneHiddenDialogTimers() clear stale forceMount timers
  |                 +-- __agentContinueResetDialogTimers() deletes dialog keys on reset
  |-- Observer --> content.js: MutationObserver (+characterData, +aria-label/data-testid/data-message-id/state/style)
  |                 +-- dynamic __agentContinueClassifyDialog + dynamic isDotPage()/getEditorSelector()
  |                 +-- SCAN_DIALOG_CONTEXT reset clears scanTimer/scanTimerTargetAt/epoch/latches/cooldowns/maskRetries
  |                 +-- non-recursive isElementVisible (no aria-hidden, 0x0 rect guard) + adapter resolveMainEditor parity
  |                 +-- isVisibleNonOverlay() (excludes overlay & sidebar; keeps <header>/<footer> for composer/handover)
  |                 +-- isIdleForTakeover() (no Stop; on Dot .typing-indicator ok if filtered main composer Send present)
  |                 +-- decoupled: ROUTINE_APPROVAL_OBSERVED AND observeIdleTakeoverPrompt(); scanInFlight mutex + epoch checks
  |                 +-- stripClosedSignInLines + row data-message-id unwrap + full-text captchaPattern; bounded transient retry
[Service Worker & Verification]
  |-- background.js: initialize() prunes legacy 'running' entries (uniform {timedOut} 250ms race + diagnosticStorageWrite)
  |                 +-- persistDeliveryTrace(): awaits diagnosticStorageWrite if uncleaned; separate get('deliveryDiagnostics'),
  |                 |   (sequence, startedAt) dedup, incidentDiscardPrevious, startedAt||sequence sort, post-write commit
  |                 +-- handlePagePromptObserved() order: sender -> enabled -> tab/url -> opt-in -> cap -> phase (transient:true)
  |                 +-- strict retryable handled:false ONLY on transient or auto-allowed-prompt; all settled idle paths handled:true
  |                 +-- finishRoundLocked() default takeoverTrigger, resume() cleanup, SW wake reset:true, healthy-only idle ping
  |-- adapter.js: ancestor visibility/hidden/inert/closed in visible(); [hidden]/[inert] + sidebar filters; findApprovalPrompt slice
                    +-- findDotSignInCard() overlay/sidebar/strictCaptcha/userCount parity; checkTakeoverConversationPrompt() lastUser+Send
```

### Component Contracts

#### 1. Incident Telemetry Filter, Init Pruner & Reconciler (`background.js`)

- **`isIncidentTrace(trace)` (`background.js:197-204`)**: Return `false` when `!trace || typeof trace !== 'object' || Array.isArray(trace) || trace.state === 'running'`. Settled failure/degraded/interrupted/takeover/manual/counter traces (`trace.state !== 'sent'`, `trace.reason === 'takeover-replied'`, or positive incident counters) continue returning `true`. (`activity-export.js:182` and `tests/delivery-diagnostics-tests.mjs:406` explicitly test direct `'running'` passthrough in `deliveries`, while `tests/delivery-diagnostics-tests.mjs:446, 476` passes `state: 'blocked'` in `incidentDeliveries`; therefore `activity-export.js` and `delivery-diagnostics.js` are unchanged.)
- **Module-scoped flags & helper placement (`background.js:93`, `L294`)**: Declare `let incidentDeliveriesCleaned = false;` and `let incidentDiscardPrevious = false;` immediately after `diagnosticDiscardPrevious` (`L93`, outside the `readSource` / `writerSource` extraction regex ranges in `tests/background-state-tests.mjs:13-14`). Place helper `raceWithTimeout(promise, ms = 250)` outside `background.js:104-134` (e.g., right after `L294` or locally inside `initialize()`) using a uniform sentinel return (never throwing on timeout):
  `{ timedOut: true }` on timeout vs `{ timedOut: false, value, error: null }` or `{ timedOut: false, error }` on settle, clearing its `setTimeout` handle in `finally`.
- **`clearDeliveryDiagnostics()` (`background.js:206-227`)**: Set `diagnosticDiscardPrevious = true; incidentDiscardPrevious = true; incidentDeliveriesCleaned = false;`. Inside `diagnosticClearBarrier`'s `.then`: if `epoch === diagnosticEpoch`, reset `diagnosticDiscardPrevious = false; incidentDiscardPrevious = false;`.
- **Bounded, fault-isolated initialization pruning in `initialize()` (`background.js:2294-2403`)**:
  - Inside `initialize()`'s `transact(async () => ...)` **after session/resume state restoration and BEFORE all early `return;` recovery branches (`L2376` `activation-interrupted`, `L2383` `recoverPause`, `L2391` `syncAlarm` failure)**, call an inline `try { ... } catch { diagnosticFailure(initDiagnosticEpoch); }` block that **cannot hang `stateQueue` or reject `initialize()`**, and never leaves an untracked `set`:
    - Capture `const initDiagnosticEpoch = diagnosticEpoch;`.
    - Step 1: `const barrierRes = await raceWithTimeout(diagnosticClearBarrier.catch(() => undefined), 250);` — if `barrierRes.timedOut || barrierRes.error || initDiagnosticEpoch !== diagnosticEpoch || incidentDiscardPrevious`, call `diagnosticFailure(initDiagnosticEpoch)` if `barrierRes.timedOut` and `return` out of the init-prune helper (do NOT issue a speculative read after a timed-out barrier!).
    - Step 2: `const readRes = await raceWithTimeout(readUntilInvalidated(() => chrome.storage.local.get('incidentDeliveries'), diagnosticReadInvalidation), 250);` — if `readRes.timedOut || readRes.error || readRes.value?.invalidated || readRes.value?.error || initDiagnosticEpoch !== diagnosticEpoch || incidentDiscardPrevious`, call `diagnosticFailure(initDiagnosticEpoch)` **at most once** if `readRes.timedOut || readRes.error || readRes.value?.error` and `return` out of the init-prune helper!
    - Step 3: On clean read in `initDiagnosticEpoch`: let `rawInit = readRes.value?.value?.incidentDeliveries`.
      - If `rawInit === undefined`: perform **zero** storage writes and set `incidentDeliveriesCleaned = true;`.
      - If `rawInit !== undefined`: sanitize via `const cleanedInit = (Array.isArray(rawInit) ? rawInit : []).map(sanitizeDeliveryDiagnostics).filter(Boolean).filter(item => item.state !== 'running' && isIncidentTrace(item)).sort((a, b) => a.startedAt - b.startedAt || a.sequence - b.sequence).slice(-20);`. If `!Array.isArray(rawInit) || JSON.stringify(cleanedInit) !== JSON.stringify(rawInit)`:
        - Re-check `if (initDiagnosticEpoch !== diagnosticEpoch || incidentDiscardPrevious) return;` immediately before writing.
        - Create `const initWrite = chrome.storage.local.set({ incidentDeliveries: cleanedInit });` and assign **`diagnosticStorageWrite = initWrite;`** (so `clearDeliveryDiagnostics()` at `L219` and `persistDeliveryTrace()` wait for any admitted slow write before modifying `incidentDeliveries`, preventing late write resurrection!).
        - `const writeRes = await raceWithTimeout(initWrite, 250);`
        - If `writeRes.timedOut || writeRes.error`: throw (caught once by local `catch` -> `diagnosticFailure(initDiagnosticEpoch)` while keeping `incidentDeliveriesCleaned = false` for later `persistDeliveryTrace()` reconciliation). Otherwise, if still in `initDiagnosticEpoch`, set `incidentDeliveriesCleaned = true;`.
      - Else (array already normalized/clean): if still in `initDiagnosticEpoch`, set `incidentDeliveriesCleaned = true;`.
- **`persistDeliveryTrace()` (`background.js:231-293`)**:
  - Preserve `readUntilInvalidated(() => chrome.storage.local.get('deliveryDiagnostics'), signal)` as an unchanged single-string lookup so mock-storage tests in `tests/background-v2-tests.mjs:2570, 4666, 4683` remain compatible.

    1. **Stale epoch guard**: `if (incResult.invalidated || currentEpoch !== diagnosticEpoch) continue;` (aborts the entire loop iteration cleanly before `chrome.storage.local.set`).

Include `incidentUpdate = { incidentDeliveries: nextInc }` iff `freshIncidents.length > 0 || rawChanged`.
  - **Uniform post-write commit ordering**: Execute `diagnosticStorageWrite = write; await write;` inside `try`. Only AFTER `await write` resolves and while `currentEpoch === diagnosticEpoch`: always clear `diagnosticDiscardPrevious = false;`, and ONLY if `incReadSucceeded` (`!incResult.error`), set `incidentDeliveriesCleaned = true; incidentDiscardPrevious = false;`.

## 3. Alternatives Considered

- **Combine `get('deliveryDiagnostics')` and `get('incidentDeliveries')` into a single array `.get([...])` call**: Rejected because existing tests in `tests/background-v2-tests.mjs:2570, 4666, 4683` intercept `chrome.storage.local.get` checking `key === 'deliveryDiagnostics'` as an exact string.
- **Deduplicate `incidentDeliveries` by `sequence` only (`!incIds.has(item.sequence)`), or against all `fresh` traces (`ids`)**: Rejected because `newDeliveryTrace()` seeds `diagnosticSequence` solely from `deliveryDiagnostics`; after a reset, matching non-incident `ids` OR a new sequence-1 incident would overwrite an older settled `sequence: 1` incident. Keying on composite `` `${item.sequence}:${item.startedAt}` `` (`freshIncidents` only) preserves historical incidents across resets AND colliding sequences while still updating same-trace retries cleanly.
- **Sort `nextInc` by `sequence` only**: Rejected because if `diagnosticSequence` resets below retained historical entries (`seq 1 < seq 10..29`), `sort((a, b) => a.sequence - b.sequence).slice(-20)` immediately evicts new incidents. Sorting by `a.startedAt - b.startedAt || a.sequence - b.sequence` preserves chronological retention.
- **Check `pointer-events: none` on ancestors in `isVisibleBlockingDialog()`**: Rejected because CSS `pointer-events` is inherited and portal containers routinely use `pointer-events: none` around `pointer-events: auto` `[role="dialog"]` children; checking `node === dialog` handles both inherited and directly disabled dialogs accurately.
- **Check `aria-hidden="true"` inside composer/turn `visible()` / `isElementVisible()`**: Rejected because Radix/React portals can set `aria-hidden="true"` on the background `#__next` wrapper when rendering benign non-blocking overlays (Citation drawers, etc.), matching `editor-commit.js:102` and restricting `aria-hidden="true"` checks solely to `isVisibleBlockingDialog()`.
- **Exclude `header, footer` globally in `isVisibleNonOverlay()`**: Rejected because chat composers often live in `<footer>` and handover `Return control` buttons can live in `<header>`/`<footer>`; restricting `header, footer, [role="banner"]` exclusion to `hasUnansweredRoutineToolCard()` and `isVisibleBlockingDialog()` preserves handover & composer detection.
